"What does it look like from a practicable standpoint -- and I literally want your description of when you cancel a permit, you know, you've got this group of guys out there working, and then let's say somebody cancels a permit. What happens? They all walk to the parking lot, to their trucks and drive home? Or what's the protocol?"
BENJAMIN GIBSON
"I'd say -- I mean, it's Shell (SHELL USA, INC. and SHELL CHEMICAL, L.P.)'s prerogative, if they want to stay on-site, go to the lunch tent, go to their contractor trailer, whatever. But it's -- you're not authorized in the process area anymore because there are LOTOs being broken or released. When a permit is canceled, you're no longer authorized for entry into that location. And that's part of the release from LOTO, is that personnel are safely removed before you energize or manipulate to break that LOTO. So that's what -- but it could be they say, "Go home, we're going to figure this out," or it could be, "Hang at the lunch tent.""
