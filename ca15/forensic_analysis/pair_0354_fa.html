BENJAMIN GIBSON · Witness
"It's my opinion that the permit should have been pulled as soon as anybody's talking about breaking a lock. Breaking a lock is so crazy and taboo for Mr. Ferrell to, like, proceed in that manner. It's, you know -- in normal industrial environment, in my experience, if you leave a lock on, they're not just going to break it. Even if you're gone and leave the jobsite and quit, they're going to hunt you down and try to get you to come back and unlock it in the proper manner because this is so taboo. You don't do it because you could still be in the facility. So I think that's the framework you've got to view this at, not just could they stop it. I mean, anybody could do anything at any point. But there's the requirements of the industry standard and Shell (SHELL USA, INC. and SHELL CHEMICAL, L.P.)'s procedures that are really the gaps here and the breakdowns."
