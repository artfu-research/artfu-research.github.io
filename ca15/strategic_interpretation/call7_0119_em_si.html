Strategic Summarylive_help
The witness clarified that isolation points are both a pre‑existing isolation status and a planning step for blind installation, and that permits must list "Cleaned, Deinventoried, Isolated." This establishes a clear procedural prerequisite that the defendants should have met. Prosecution can use this to argue that any failure to isolate before blind work constitutes a safety violation. However, the witness does not confirm whether isolation actually occurred, so the defense can still claim compliance.
