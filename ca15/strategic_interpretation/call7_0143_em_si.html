Strategic Summaryⓘ
The witness clarified that Shell’s DSM standard mandates *positive proof* of bleeder clearance, not merely opening the valve, and that this step was missed. This confirms a procedural lapse that the prosecution can exploit to show Shell failed to meet its safety obligations. The fact that other verification methods are not required does not absolve Shell of the proof requirement, but it does give the defense a potential angle that must be countered. The prosecution should use this exchange to focus on documentation of the missed proof step and to underscore the seriousness of non‑compliance.
