Strategic Summary · Opposition Viewlive_help
The witness confirms that the defendants performed the nitrogen purge and steam cleaning in May, establishing a factual timeline that counters the plaintiff’s claim of procedural failure. However, the witness also notes that product remained after cleaning, which could be leveraged by the plaintiff to argue incomplete cleaning. The defense should use the confirmation to reinforce compliance, while preparing to counter the residual product claim as a normal outcome or insufficient evidence of negligence.
