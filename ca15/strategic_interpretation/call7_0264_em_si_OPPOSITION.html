Strategic Summary · Opposition Viewⓘ
The witness clarified that canceling a permit revokes entry authorization, triggers LOTO release, and that Shell retains prerogative over worker location. This demonstrates that the defendants followed the required safety protocol for permit cancellation, which the defense can use to counter the plaintiff’s claim of negligence. The only risk is that the description of Shell’s prerogative could be spun by the opposition to suggest they had control but failed to act. Walk into this pair knowing that the testimony is a strong asset for showing compliance, but be ready to pre‑empt any framing that frames Shell’s authority as a liability.
