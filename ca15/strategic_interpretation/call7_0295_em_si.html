Strategic Summaryⓘ
The defense’s question about contractors’ awareness was met with a request for a specific stage, shifting the focus from a general awareness inquiry to a precise temporal frame. This ‘Temporal Shift’ gives the defense a tool to narrow the scope of the witness’s testimony and potentially avoid admitting that the defendants failed to follow safety practices. For the prosecution, the key is to turn that temporal focus into evidence that the defendants were unaware of critical safety protocols at the relevant stage, while also preparing to counter the defense’s use of the clarification to dodge the core issue.
