Strategic Summaryⓘ

This exchange provides factual support that Shell’s safety procedures were invoked (or possibly breached) during the incident. The prosecution can use it to show that Shell’s operational decisions—specifically the motor use and LOTO handling—were central to the event and may reflect a failure to adhere to proper safety practices. It is a low‑intensity, routine confirmation, but the fact that the decision was made and recorded is strategically valuable.
