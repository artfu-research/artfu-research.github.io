Strategic Summaryⓘ
The witness clarified that bumping a valve is intended to clear obstructions and that an open downstream is a safety feature. This exchange gives the prosecution a foothold to argue that the defendants failed to maintain proper safety practices, specifically that the downstream safety feature was absent or improperly operated. However, the defense can also use the confirmation that bumping was performed to suggest that the defendants followed standard procedures. The key is to probe whether the downstream was truly open at the time of the incident and whether the valve was properly maintained.
