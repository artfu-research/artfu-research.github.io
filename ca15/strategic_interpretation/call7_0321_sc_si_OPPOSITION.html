Strategic Summary · Opposition Viewlive_help
The expert confirms that Shell USA has a formal policy prohibiting the bumping of valves and mandates lock‑out/tag‑out procedures. This establishes that a safety protocol existed, which can be leveraged to argue that the defendants had the necessary safeguards in place. However, the witness does not testify that Shell actually followed the policy in the incident, so the evidence is limited to policy existence rather than compliance. The defense should be prepared to use this fact to counter claims of negligence while noting the lack of direct evidence of non‑compliance.
