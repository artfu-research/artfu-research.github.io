Strategic Summary · Opposition Viewlive_help
The expert confirms that Mr. Ferrell was aware of multiple pathways to the atmosphere and that the valve is the sole isolation point. This shows the defendants had a single point of control but multiple routes to release, a fact the plaintiff can exploit to claim inadequate safety. The defense should prepare to challenge the significance of these pathways and the adequacy of the valve, while also highlighting that the expert’s testimony does not itself prove a safety failure.
