This focus shift means the witness cannot substantiate the plaintiff’s claim that Shell failed to follow proper safety practices, and the defense can argue that the testimony is incomplete and biased toward the plaintiff’s narrative. The witness’s technical, emotion‑free language and critique of company procedures further support a strategy that questions his credibility and relevance. Before proceeding, the defense should be prepared to highlight the shift and the narrow scope of the expert’s opinion to neutralize any attempt by opposing counsel to use this testimony to shift liability away from the plaintiff.
