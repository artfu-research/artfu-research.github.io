Strategic Summary · Opposition Viewlive_help
The witness confirms that Shell issued a permit for work involving a 14‑inch outlet and that the work required pulling bundles, breaking air gapping, and disconnecting piping. This anchors the defendants’ actions to a documented permit and standard engineering procedures, supporting the defense’s claim that safety protocols were followed. However, the mention of breaking air gapping could be leveraged by the plaintiff to argue a safety lapse, so it must be monitored. Walk into the next pair knowing that this exchange provides a solid, though not decisive, technical foothold for the defense.
