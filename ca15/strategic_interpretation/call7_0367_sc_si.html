Strategic Summarylive_help
The witness confirms that the MOV‑2516 valve was closed and that the closure involved the bottom inlet piping, but also notes that the action broke regulatory rules and was linked to an unfortunate event. This pair offers a direct admission of regulatory non‑compliance that can be leveraged to argue the defendants failed to follow proper safety practices. However, the witness also confirms the valve was isolated, which the defense could use to claim an attempt at compliance. The prosecution should therefore highlight the regulatory breach and the cited accident while countering any narrative that the defendants merely complied.
