Strategic Summary · Opposition Viewlive_help
The witness clarified that the photo was taken after the safety walk but before the incident and before permits were issued. This new temporal detail can be leveraged to show that the walk was conducted under proper conditions, but it also exposes the fact that safety protocols were not yet formally in place, a point the plaintiff can exploit. The defense should be prepared to use the photo’s timing to argue that the conditions observed were normal and that the defendants complied with industry standards, while also being ready to counter any argument that the lack of permits indicates a lapse in safety practice.
