Strategic Summary · Opposition Viewⓘ
The witness identified specific personnel who were aware of the extreme risk and cited Shell’s own documents that require control of hazardous energy and adherence to process‑safety standards. However, he also admitted that the steps taken were wrong and that a rogue local practice existed. For the defense this means we have concrete proof that protocols were in place and documented, but also that the defendants may have deviated from them – a double‑edged sword that can be used to argue that the plaintiff’s negligence, not a failure of safety practice, caused the incident.
