Strategic Summary · Opposition Viewⓘ

For the defense, this exchange is a double‑edged sword—on one hand it furnishes a ready‑made narrative that the defendants were negligent; on the other, it is rife with bias, lack of corroboration, and exaggeration that we can exploit. Walk into this pair prepared to dissect the missing evidence links, highlight the attorney’s leverage, and expose the amplified, subjective claims. The goal is to neutralize the impact of Gibson’s testimony and shift the burden back onto the plaintiff.
